World-space field — real feet, one projection

Every point (bases, fielders, wall vertices) is defined in real feet from home plate, then run through a single project(). A throw is just a vector between two world points (see the dashed RF → cutoff → home line). Compare the scale (Little League vs MLB) and how the projection handles the tall phone: FIT shows every pixel with letterboxing; FILL covers the screen but crops the foul corners.
